## Gorchev - "Princes"

The Prince carries his chosen one into a happier world; the moment that is done, he stops being a Prince and what remains is a household pet. The whole of Kernberg's "Love Relations" in a single short story - in Manyuk's opinion.

## Gorchev - "Time"

Grandmother's wall clock is a mechanism for producing slow, slow time; electronic time tells you only how late you already are.
