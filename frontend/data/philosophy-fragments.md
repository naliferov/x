## Protagoras (sophist)

"On every matter there are two opinions opposed to each other."  

My thought latched onto this. Reflecting reality is, one way or another, a reflection of one's own reality. This lets you sense that automatic contraband that has been smuggled into us and set off at the necessary moment. Contraband about how to reflect and carry something across in such a way that others, too, can reflect it. This utilitarianism hums within us, feeding the skill of survival, and accordingly, once that task of survival is accomplished, it drifts on in the form of enslaving other movements by this movement — or of reflection... One reflection for the sake of another, one for the other, being for the other.

## Vadim Rudnev

We look for repetitions and find differences, and when we look for differences, we find nothing but repetitions

## Сверки с [усилием](/doc/mamardashvili)

**Merleau-Ponty.** **The body schema** — the body is not one object among objects but the vehicle of being-in-the-world. I do not *have* a body and steer it; I *am* my body, and motor intentionality ("I can") runs ahead of any representation. The shift is from consciousness to the lived body — the antidote to reading [Mamardashvili's effort](/doc/mamardashvili) as a purely intellectual act.

**Sartre.** **Bad faith (mauvaise foi)** — self-deception with no second person to do the deceiving: fleeing freedom by playing a thing with a fixed essence. The waiter who *is* a waiter; the woman who leaves her hand in his and treats it as an object. Almost the exact antipode of [effort](/doc/mamardashvili) — the refusal of the labor of choosing oneself.

**Simone Weil.** Closest in intonation to [Mamardashvili's effort](/doc/mamardashvili), in an entirely different vocabulary — and pointed the other way: at emptying rather than at holding.

**Attention** — the highest effort and yet a negative one: not muscular straining but suspending thought, holding it empty and available, so the object can enter. "Attention, taken to its highest degree, is the same thing as prayer."

**Levinas.** Against the whole line above.

> The sharpest counterpoint to [Mamardashvili](/doc/mamardashvili): there, effort is solitary and the courage of the impossible presupposes fundamental solitude. Here nothing is saved alone.

**Pyatigorsky.** Nearest of the contemporaries to [Mamardashvili](/doc/mamardashvili) by type of thinking; together they wrote **"Symbol and Consciousness"** (1982).
